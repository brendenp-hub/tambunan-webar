TAMBUNAN FIELD GUIDE

How to scan

  1. Use any story page from 1 to 12. Lay it flat with the bottom edge facing you, or open its printable target on another screen.
  2. Tap Start camera and allow access. Hold your camera above the page so all four corners are visible.
  3. The scanner picks the scene automatically. Turn to another supported page without stopping or choosing a page in the app. Keep the page in view. Tap objects directly, or use the controls below the view. For a more angled view, tilt your phone while keeping the page visible. Drag left or right to turn the diorama, or up and down to tilt it.

Use 3D preview to explore without a camera. On a phone, the scanner must be opened on an HTTPS site.
